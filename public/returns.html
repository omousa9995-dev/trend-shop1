Returns and delivery policy

This policy applies to Trend Shop orders within Jordan.

  • Unopened packaging: request a return within 7 days of receiving the order.
  • Opened packaging: request a return within 24 hours of receiving the order. Opened packaging is not accepted after this period.
  • Wrong product or store error: the store pays return shipping once the error is verified.
  • Returns without a store error, such as a change of mind: the customer pays return shipping.

Contact the team with your order number and an explanation to request a return or discuss an exchange. Return shipping is coordinated with Trend Shop.

Handling and delivery operate every day. The daily order cutoff is 23:00, Amman time. Delivery throughout Jordan is within 24 hours of order confirmation.

Delivery is 1 JOD when the discounted product subtotal is 19 JOD or less. Delivery is free when that subtotal is more than 19 JOD.
