# Luma conversation — v5.16.0

Luma (لُمى) is the store's named automated shopping assistant. The customer starts with their own words and can optionally add an image of their own adult face, hands, feet, hair or non-intimate body skin. A short automated-assistant disclosure remains visible; image processing consent names Google. The interface does not present Luma as a human or clinician.

The old up-front questionnaire becomes an optional preferences drawer. Gemini understands the conversation, resolves the requested area and asks at most two relevant follow-up questions. Customers can type freely or choose validated quick replies. A budget extracted from natural language must be confirmed before product cards appear. Changing a confirmed budget or area also requires a choice; model output cannot silently expand the spending limit or switch the target area.

The live catalog now covers approved, active, in-stock skin, body, hair and makeup products. Gemini receives documented facts for up to 350 eligible products, without the prior local keyword preference scoring. Server checks still enforce area taxonomy, confirmed budget, shipping, current stock and prices, real SKU IDs, complementary product types and existing cart exclusions. Candidate types include ordinary cosmetic toners, serums and body/hair products as well as basic facial care. Medicines, retinoids, exfoliating acids and active-treatment candidates remain excluded. Product reasons are model-generated against supplied facts and are not independently validated clinical claims. Exact use directions come from the store's saved product data.

The shopping scope does not include diagnosis or treatment from photos. Visible observations are tentative and bounded. Concerning signs lead to professional assessment without product sales. Unsupported photos return no recommendations and can be removed to continue the conversation. These safeguards do not establish medical reliability or validate image accuracy.

A floating "Ask Luma" / "اسألي لُمى" entry is shown only after actual configuration is ready. It avoids checkout and visible modal overlays and is positioned above mobile purchase controls. The footer and category rail share the name. Observers pause on page hide and resume after back-forward cache restoration without a rendering loop. Product detail links open separately so the live conversation can remain available during review.

The Google project setup from v5.15.0 is retained: encrypted owner-entered key, owner-confirmed paid project, explicit activation, separate optional images control, per-IP throttle, atomic daily cap, bounded output, no automatic retries and aggregate metrics only. No API key or billing was created or activated in this release. There is no model training on customer content. The store saves no photos, transcripts or profile records. The browser retains a short in-memory conversation only, strips uploaded photo metadata and clears conversation/image state when leaving the page.

Validation covers budget-free natural starts, model-extracted budget confirmation, cross-area SKU rejection, budget/area changes, clarification priority, unsupported photos, bounded catalog inclusion, conversational quick replies and launcher lifecycle. Existing purchase, search, admin and security checks remain required. Interface QA uses clearly marked isolated synthetic responses. Real Gemini answers, real photo interpretation and physical iPhone behavior remain unverified until the owner supplies a suitable key and paid-project setup.
